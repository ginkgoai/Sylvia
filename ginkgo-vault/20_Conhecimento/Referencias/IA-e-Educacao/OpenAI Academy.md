Courses

![Card image](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/1a-100-ebe81901-79ed-4ccf-8496-62fa0e65aeea-1781195101360.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=700)

Great for people new to AI

AI Foundations

Learn the basics of AI, large language models, and ChatGPT. You’ll practice giving clear instructions, adding useful context, reviewing outputs, and using AI responsibly in everyday work.

![Card image](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/2a-100-b8a85d37-4bb7-4a87-ae10-381881946e23-1781195124818.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=700)

Great for people who already have some experience

Applied AI Foundations

Turn a recurring task into a repeatable workflow. You’ll break the work into steps, identify where ChatGPT can help, and build review points into the process.

![Card image](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/3a-100-5b39c872-b7e9-4431-b0cd-e1b4f5b5c09d-1781195136157.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=700)

For people comfortable using AI

Agents and Workflows

Practice directing agents through structured work. You’ll provide context, define outputs, set boundaries, review drafts, improve the workflow, and reuse what works.
