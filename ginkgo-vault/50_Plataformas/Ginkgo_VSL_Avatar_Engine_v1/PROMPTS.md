# Prompt Pack

## Retrato mestre

Ultra-photorealistic chest-up portrait of an adult Brazilian technology
presenter, direct eye contact, relaxed neutral lips, mouth fully visible,
natural skin texture and asymmetry, individual hair strands, plain matte
black blouse, soft frontal window light, neutral blurred interior, no beauty
filter, no glossy fabric, no dramatic shadow over the mouth, camera at eye
level, realistic photography.

Negative:
CGI, illustration, plastic skin, glossy blouse, hair covering lips, strong
smile, open mouth, excessive makeup, dramatic pose, tilted face, deep shadow,
identity distortion.

## Vídeo-guia

Fixed camera at eye level. Direct eye contact, small blinks, subtle breathing,
tiny head movements, restrained eyebrow emphasis and no hand gestures. The
body remains almost still.

## Atuação

Speak as if talking privately to one specific person. Calm, confident and
helpful. Use micro-pauses after the hook and before the CTA. Avoid presenter
voice, exaggerated smile and theatrical expression.

## Legendas

- duas linhas no máximo;
- 3 a 7 palavras por bloco;
- destacar uma palavra estratégica;
- não cobrir boca, queixo ou CTA.
